How a session works

Answer, see what to fix, answer again.

  1. 01

    Bring the job, or pick a role

    Paste a job post link and get questions built from its requirements. Or practise from a bank of hand-written questions by role and seniority.

    jobs.example.com/senior-frontend-engineer
  2. 02

    Answer out loud

    The interviewer reads the question. You get 15 seconds to think, then you speak and watch the transcript. Fix misheard words before it’s scored.

    so I split the migration into three…
  3. 03

    Try again until it lands

    Every answer gets six scores, quotes from what you said and one rewrite to try. Answer again and see exactly what moved.

What the feedback looks at

Six things interviewers listen for.

Pace, filler words and pauses are counted in your browser, not by the language model.

Structure
Situation, action, result in order. Or definition, example, trade-offs.
Specificity
Systems, numbers, constraints, tool names.
Ownership
What you did, said with “I”, apart from the team.
Impact
The result, measured where possible.
Relevance
Whether you answered the question asked.
Clarity
Whether a listener follows it the first time.
